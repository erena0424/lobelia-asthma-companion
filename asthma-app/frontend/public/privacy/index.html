Lobelia

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lobelia (“Lobelia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Lobelia website and related services. Lobelia is a research prototype built by a student developer.

Lobelia is a personal asthma self-management companion. It combines what you log with weather, air quality and your planned activities to offer short, educational self-management tips.

Lobelia is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. It does not replace professional medical advice or your asthma action plan.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address
  • Password, stored in hashed form
  • Name and other optional profile information
  • Profile photo, if you choose to add one
  • Date of birth
  • Emergency contact information
  • Preferences, asthma triggers and self-management goals

Health and symptom information

If you use Lobelia’s tracking features, you may provide:

  • Daily symptoms and how strong they were
  • Night-time symptoms and activity limitations
  • Asthma triggers
  • Rescue inhaler use
  • Personal notes and anything else you choose to add

Location information

If you allow it in your browser or device, Lobelia receives your approximate location (latitude and longitude) to look up local weather, air quality and pollen. You can deny location access; some features then use a fallback location or work with less detail.

Google Calendar information

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Lobelia reads events on calendars you own. It creates an event only when you add a plan in Lobelia and choose to add it to Google Calendar, and changes or deletes an event only when you edit or delete it in Lobelia. Section 5 explains exactly what we access and how we use it.

Chat and AI feature information

If you use Lobelia’s chat or AI-assisted features, we process the messages you send and the context needed to answer, such as recent symptoms, local conditions, planned activities and your earlier Lobelia records. Chat messages are used to generate a reply and are not stored as a conversation history in Lobelia’s database.

2. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate and manage your account, and send account emails
  • Record the symptoms and other information you choose to log
  • Show patterns across your own logs and offer self-management tips
  • Take local conditions and your planned activities into account in those tips
  • Retrieve relevant information from your earlier records to personalize replies
  • Provide the Google Calendar connection when you turn it on
  • Operate, maintain and secure Lobelia, and respond to support requests

3. Service providers

Lobelia uses these providers to run its features:

  • Resend: account verification and sign-in emails.
  • Google Gemini API and Anthropic Claude: writing tips and chat replies. We use these services under paid terms that do not permit the provider to train its models on the content we send.
  • Open-Meteo and OpenWeather: weather and related conditions.
  • Google Pollen: pollen levels.
  • Google Calendar API: the optional calendar connection.
  • Cloudinary: profile photos, if you add one.
  • Google Cloud and Vercel: hosting the website, servers and database.

Depending on the feature, health-related context, local conditions, planned activities or chat messages may be sent to these providers to perform the service you asked for. We do not sell your personal information.

4. How we store information

Lobelia stores, on hosted cloud infrastructure:

  • Account information
  • Symptom logs, inhaler-use records and notes
  • Local conditions linked to your daily view
  • Tips generated for you
  • Calendar connection information and calendar details used in tips
  • Summaries or embeddings used to find relevant earlier records

5. Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. If you connect it, Lobelia asks Google for access to events on calendars you own (calendar.events.owned), so you can see your events in Lobelia, edit them there, and add plans you make in Lobelia to your Google Calendar. Lobelia only creates an event when you add a plan yourself and leave “Also add to my Google Calendar” ticked, and only changes or deletes an event when you edit or delete that event (or that plan) yourself in Lobelia; deleting asks you to confirm first. Lobelia never creates, changes or deletes your events on its own, and does not read calendars you only subscribe to.

What we read: event title, start and end time, whether it is all-day, location, description and status, for the days Lobelia shows you.

What we send to Google: when you add a plan to Google Calendar, only its name, time and location. The plan’s other details (inside or outside, how active, what might bother your breathing, and your notes) stay in Lobelia.

How we use it: only to show your plans in Lobelia, to save the plans you add and the changes you make in Lobelia to Google Calendar, and to tailor your daily self-management tips to your plans, for example noting an outdoor activity on a day with poor air quality.

Who we share it with: when a tip is generated, the relevant event details are sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini API or Anthropic) only to write that tip, under terms that do not permit the provider to train on it. We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

What we store: an OAuth refresh token so your tips can update each day, tips that may mention an event, and, for a plan you added to Google Calendar, the ID of that Google event so your edits in Lobelia reach the same event. When you disconnect Google Calendar in Lobelia, we revoke Lobelia’s access with Google and delete the token. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI training: Lobelia does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use: Lobelia’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and logged information while your account is active, or as long as needed to provide the features described here. Sign-in and verification codes expire after about 10 minutes. Tips created before you disconnect Google Calendar may still mention an event. Some information may be kept longer where needed for security or legal reasons.

7. Your choices

  • Choose whether to add optional profile and health information
  • Deny location access in your browser or device
  • Choose whether to connect Google Calendar, and disconnect it at any time
  • Update your profile and your logs
  • Ask us to delete your account and its data by emailing support@lobelia.app

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information: passwords are stored hashed, sign-in uses login tokens, data is stored on hosted cloud infrastructure with its providers’ security controls, and access to app services is limited to what is needed to run Lobelia. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Medical disclaimer

Lobelia offers educational self-management support. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, or for your asthma action plan, and it is not a medical device. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about medical decisions. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or seek emergency care right away.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Lobelia changes. We will update the date above, and if we start using your information in a new way we will ask for your consent first.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: support@lobelia.app
